# Kiba data-rights runbook

Self-service authenticated export and password-confirmed deletion handle common access/portability
and erasure requests. Profile controls handle display name and language correction. Manual fallback
is available through **support@cyberdurak.com**.

## Manual request workflow

1. Log receipt date, requested right (access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or
   objection), scope, communication channel and deadline. Store the minimum information needed.
2. Verify identity proportionately. Prefer proof through the signed-in account or a confirmation to
   its verified email. Never request a password or full authentication token by email.
3. Acknowledge the request and target response within one month of receipt where GDPR applies.
   Document any legally permitted extension and inform the requester within the original period.
4. Locate relevant account/profile, match, XP, achievement and cosmetic data; check retained PvP
   opponent records, providers, logs and backups as applicable.
5. Apply the requested action when applicable. Restriction/objection and legal-basis disputes are
   **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** before making irreversible assumptions.
6. Review output for other users' data, credentials, token digests, hidden cards, configuration and
   secrets. Provide data through an authenticated or otherwise secure channel.
7. Record completion, refusal/partial action and legal reason, communications, and any follow-up.
   Tell the requester about complaint options where applicable.

## Request notes

- **Access/export:** self-service JSON is the first route. Manual export may be needed when account
  access is unavailable after identity verification.
- **Correction:** display name and locale are self-service. Email correction is not currently
  self-service and needs identity/security review.
- **Deletion:** self-service requires the current password and explicit confirmation. Live account
  data is deleted transactionally; opponents' retained PvP summaries are anonymized. Backups rotate
  normally rather than being rewritten.
- **Restriction/objection:** record the request, prevent avoidable new processing where appropriate,
  and obtain legal review because applicability depends on basis and circumstances.
- **Children or representative requests:** do not improvise authority checks; escalate for legal
  review.

Never ask users to email passwords, cookie values, raw reset/verification links, or reconnect
credentials. Keep request logs access-restricted and define their retention during final legal
review.
