# Kiba legal-review checklist

This list records unresolved decisions rather than presenting them as settled law.

## Required before public beta

- [ ] Review controller wording and proposed legal bases with qualified counsel.
- [ ] Determine children/minimum-age position and any age-related consent/safeguarding needs.
- [ ] Review Privacy Policy, Terms, deletion/anonymization and one-month rights workflow.
- [ ] Confirm whether the final Finnish operating model requires an additional public
  geographical/business address. Do not publish a private home address automatically.
- [ ] Select Phase 6B providers; review locations, subprocessors, DPAs, transfer safeguards and
  provider retention; update `PROCESSORS.md` and the public Privacy Policy.
- [ ] Make `support@cyberdurak.com` operational and monitored.
- [ ] Configure and verify 30-day log and backup rotation; review backup-deletion/restoration
  wording and downstream deletion procedure.
- [ ] Review Terms under applicable Finnish/EU consumer and information-society rules, including
  governing-law and liability language.
- [ ] Confirm no optional analytics/advertising/marketing tracking is enabled; reassess consent if
  that changes.

## Required before monetization or commercial expansion

- [ ] Reassess Finnish business-registration, consumer, tax/VAT, mandatory trader-information and
  address requirements for the actual model.
- [ ] Add purchase/refund/payment/processor terms only if purchases are introduced.
- [ ] Review abuse/moderation, sanctions and account-retention rules at the expected scale.

## Future-market-specific

- [ ] Before deliberate RF-market launch, VK Play publication, RF-targeted marketing, `.ru`
  positioning or RF-specific production infrastructure, reassess Russian-market and data-residency
  obligations. Russian UI availability alone is not treated as a targeted launch decision.
- [ ] Reassess international transfers before any non-EEA provider or region processes personal
  data.
- [ ] Reassess privacy/cookie rules before analytics, ads, marketing pixels, social embeds or other
  non-essential tracking.
